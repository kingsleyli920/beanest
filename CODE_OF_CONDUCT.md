# Code of conduct · 行为准则

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

Beanest follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, and discussions is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work rather than the person, and do not harass, insult, or publish anyone's private information.

To report a problem, open an issue asking the maintainer for a private contact channel. Do not include the details in public. The maintainer will review reports and may edit or remove content, or block participants who break these rules.

## 简体中文

豆窝采用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。在 issue、PR 和讨论区参与的人都应遵守。

简单说：互相尊重，有耐心，先假定对方是善意的；批评针对内容，不针对人；不骚扰、不辱骂，不公开别人的私人信息。

遇到问题时，请先开一个 issue 请维护者提供私下联系方式，不要在公开区域写细节。维护者会处理举报，可以修改或删除相关内容，也可以屏蔽违规的参与者。
